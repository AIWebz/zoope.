FAQ

Questions

Will people know it's my AI avatar?

Yes. Zoope always labels your avatar as "Zoope AI" in the participant name and announces itself when it joins. Being upfront keeps everyone comfortable.

Can someone else clone my face or voice?

No. Scans require a live camera and microphone with liveness checks, and you can only create an avatar of yourself.

Where is my data stored?

Your face and voice models are encrypted and tied to your account. You can delete them at any time from settings.

Does it work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Open zoope in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, and it works like a native app.
